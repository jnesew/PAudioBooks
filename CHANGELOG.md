# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-13

Initial release of PAudioBooks for Android 8.0 and newer.

- Add local audio files or folders with one review before adding books. Original
  audio stays at its selected location and is never modified or deleted.
- Browse a list or cover grid with search, sorting, listening-state filters, and
  live genre and author filters.
- Listen in the background with system media controls, remembered progress,
  chapters, book/chapter seeking, configurable skips, and 0.50×–3.00× speed.
- Save bookmarks and notes, undo recent seeks, use a sleep timer, and enable
  optional smart rewind.
- Read embedded covers, chapters and book information; keep local metadata and
  cover edits without rewriting audio tags.
- Choose System, Light or Dark appearance and lock-screen detail visibility.
- Manually export and restore progress, bookmarks and edited details through
  reviewed audio-fingerprint matches, with bounded parsing and cancellable I/O.
- Run offline without accounts, Google Play services, ads, analytics or app
  networking. App backup/transfer is disabled; manual exports are unencrypted.

Format support depends on the audio encoding, document provider and device.
See the [release notes](docs/RELEASE-NOTES-0.1.0.md) for compatibility, installation
and initial-release limitations.
